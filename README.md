# RadarMakers

RadarMakers is a launchpad for indie makers. Products are rated on idea, execution, design and value, not on launch day hype.

Makers submit a product with a description, screenshots and links. The community browses, upvotes and comments, and every product can be rated on four axes: idea, execution, design and value.

RadarMakers is early stage and growing.

Website: https://radarmakers.com
